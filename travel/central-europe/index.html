Central Europe in 14 days

A road trip from Prague to Venice through seven countries: baroque libraries, a salt mine, alpine passes, and long walks through old towns.

Thoughts & take-aways

    About the numbers

    Driving and walking times are routing estimates for the stops in the order listed, without time spent at them; plan for more. Driving times come from OSRM, walking times from the FOSSGIS foot router, both on OpenStreetMap data. Slovakia is crossed twice on the way, with stops but no nights. Photos are from the trip.
