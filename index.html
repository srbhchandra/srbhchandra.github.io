Data Apps Travel Résumé

I'm a machine learning scientist in Mountain View, California. For the past nine years I've built ML for education — first at Gradescope as one of two ML scientists behind its grading automation, and now at Turnitin, where I tech-lead AI writing detection and built several of the LLM features in Clarity, a TIME Best Invention of 2025.

Most of my work sits where being wrong is expensive. A recommender can miss a third of the time and still be useful; a system that flags a student's writing, or matches a handwritten name to a class roster, cannot. High precision, false-positive rates that stay low and even across groups, models that know when to abstain — that's the thread through most of what I've built, and it's why I keep coming back to integrity problems. Accountability and safety, a small step at a time.

I like staying close enough to the product to hear from the people using it. This site is where I collect public work and the occasional write-up. Say hello at srbhchandra@gmail.com.
